### What still prevents an “all planned demonstrations ready” claim

1. Run the intended real model through indexing, workflow, streaming and the selected agent/graph paths; inspect answers, latency and usage. Do not substitute mock pass rates for generated-answer quality.
2. Independently review corpus/golden/held-out data and review the prepared controlled conflicting-policy example and its expected escalation. Calibrate any model judge and retrieval thresholds on reviewed cases.
3. Produce at least one same-dataset model/settings comparison report. Routing/caching savings must be measured; optional flags and a price calculator are not an experiment.
4. Confirm receipt for the actual model investigation (metadata-only collector connectivity is verified), pre-run the selected standalone provider-state comparison if shown, and rehearse the actual 60-minute sequence with recovery paths.
5. Keep later-contact/practice coverage explicitly labeled. Do not claim that long-term memory, compaction, reranking, parallel dispatch, moderation, Agents SDK or managed runtimes were demonstrated by this application.

The first fifteen minutes provide the conceptual map; the forty-five minutes execute selected prepared paths and one small live edit. All variants and appendix pages are not extra live build tasks. Actual timed rehearsal must establish whether that pacing works.
