# Redelivery after missed collection

A recipient who cannot collect a held parcel may ask the support team to arrange one redelivery attempt before the seven-day collection period ends. The support team must verify the recipient and confirm the delivery address first.

A supervisor must approve the redelivery request. Asking for redelivery does not extend the collection deadline. Agents must not promise a confirmed delivery slot until dispatch confirms it.

This synthetic policy does not authorize a payment, service credit or automatic dispatch action.
